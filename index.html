Research

  1. Galois Groups of Trinomials with Small Coefficients
    2026
    Abs arXiv

    We calculate the Galois group of $f = x^n + ax^k + b \in \mathbb{Z}\left[ x \right]$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ for all $1 \leq \left| ab \right| \leq 3$, $n$ sufficiently large and $k$ coprime to $n$. In particular, we prove that the Galois group of $f$ is the maximal one that its factorization type allows. We give a criterion for the Galois group of a factor of a trinomial to be symmetric, with an eye towards future applications. To the best of our knowledge, the only previously known explicit example of polynomials of degree $n$ with bounded coefficients and Galois group $S_n$ is $x^n - x - 1$, while recent results show that the probability that a random polynomial of degree $n$ with reasonably bounded coefficients has Galois group $S_n$ tends to $1$ as $n$ tends to infinity. Using our calculation, one can construct many new such explicit examples.

  2. Fields Generated by a Classifying Invariant of CM Bielliptic Picard Curves
    2024
    Abs PDF

    We calculate the fields generated by certain curves of the kind $C \colon y^3 = x^4 + ax^2 + b$ in the following way: The endomorphism ring of the associated Prym variety $P$ of this kind of curves admits an embedding of a quaternion order $\mathcal{O}$, and we are interested in the cases where the centralizer of $\mathcal{O}$ in $\operatorname{End}(P)$ is a maximal order of an imaginary quadratic field. We characterize the imaginary quadratic fields, $K$, whose maximal order is the centralizer of $\mathcal{O}$ in $\operatorname{End}(P)$ for some $C$ of the above kind, and we calculated the field generated over $K$ by a classifying invariant of the curves of the above kind in terms of class field theory.

Conference talks

  1. Galois Groups of Trinomials with Small Coefficients
    2026
    Award Abs Extended Abstract Slides

    I won the 2026 IMU Student Talk

    Hilbert's Irreducibility Theorem (1892) implies that the Galois group over the rationals of "almost" any polynomial $f$ with rational coefficients is $S_n$. However, it is very difficult to find explicit examples of polynomials with Galois group $S_n$. To our knowledge, the only previously known explicit example with bounded integer coefficients is the family of trinomials $x^n - x - 1$, which was proven by Nart and Vila (1979). We proved a few new families of polynomials with bounded integer coefficients to have Galois group $S_n$ for any $n$. In this talk, I will sketch the proof of the known result of Nart and Vila, before presenting the approach we used to prove our new results. To illustrate our method, I will use the example of the family $x^n - 2x + 1$, which factors as the product of $x-1$ and the generalized Fibonacci polynomial $x^{n-1} + x^{n-2} + \cdots + x - 1$, which was shown to have Galois group $S_{n-1}$ for odd values of $n$ by Martin (2004) and for even values of $n$ by us (2026). Along the way, I will define inertia groups, use properties of the $p$-adic numbers, and give a taste of Grothendieck's Galois theory.
